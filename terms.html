TERMS & NOTICE.

A straightforward explanation of what BLACKSITE is, what it is not, and how this fictional experience should be used.

DEVELOPER / Tommy Jenkins  ·  UPDATED / November 7, 2026

1. About the experience

A51 BLACKSITE is independent interactive fiction created by Tommy Jenkins. It offers a custom Chrome new-tab page with a fictional classified investigation, simulated hacker terminal, cinematic puzzles, and optional local utilities.

2. Fiction and safety

Every government file, restricted system, intercepted signal, simulated privilege escalation, security camera, radar display, and anomalous event in the story is fictional. BLACKSITE does not provide access to protected systems or give its users actual government clearance or administrator privileges.

3. Software and availability

BLACKSITE is provided for entertainment and personal use. Features may be updated or discontinued. Browser behavior, local storage settings, and external destinations can affect operation. Back up your story progress before reinstalling or resetting browser data.

4. Privacy and external services

Please read the Privacy Policy for details about local saved data, default-provider web searches, backup files, and user-initiated external links. Google Maps, Google Earth, and other external websites operate under their own terms and privacy policies.
